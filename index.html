Will Fitness Privacy Policy Last updated: August 29, 2026 Will Fitness is a free privacy-focused fitness and nutrition application developed by an independent developer. The app is designed to keep your information on your device. 1. Information stored by Will Fitness Will Fitness stores information you enter or create while using the app, such as workout information, nutrition information, food entries, preferences, and other app data. This information is stored locally on your device. Will Fitness does not operate a user account system or a server where this information is uploaded or stored. 2. Food barcode lookups When you scan or manually enter a food barcode and request product information, Will Fitness may send that barcode to Open Food Facts to retrieve information about the corresponding food product. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts may receive the IP address associated with the request as part of the network connection. Will Fitness does not send your workout history, nutrition logs, or other personal data to Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts receives the request under its own privacy practices. 3. Camera Will Fitness requests camera access solely to scan food barcodes. Camera images are processed for this purpose within the app. Will Fitness does not upload photographs or camera footage. 4. Notifications and workout timer Will Fitness may use Android notifications and a foreground service while a workout timer is running. This allows the timer to continue operating when you switch apps or put Will Fitness in the background. The workout timer does not upload your workout information to Will Fitness. 5. Contacting the developer If you choose to contact the developer via email at WillFitnessapp@gmail.com, your message is sent through your standard email client. Emails you voluntarily send may be used to respond to your feedback, support request, or other message. You may contact the developer to request deletion of email correspondence. 6. Exporting your data Will Fitness allows you to export your locally stored app data. If you choose to share an export, Android's sharing system allows you to send the exported file to another application or service of your choice. Will Fitness does not automatically upload these exports. 7. Your data Because your Will Fitness data is stored locally and Will Fitness does not maintain user accounts or a server containing your data, the developer does not have access to your locally stored app data. If you remove Will Fitness and its local files from your device, the locally stored data will no longer be available through the app. Any copies you have separately exported or stored elsewhere are unaffected. 8. Changes to this policy This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of Will Fitness changes. The latest version will be made available with the application. 9. Contact For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have voluntarily sent to the developer, contact: WillFitnessapp@gmail.com
