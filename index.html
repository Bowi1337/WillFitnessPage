Why Will Fitness?

When I wanted to get into shape, I downloaded the most popular nutrition and workout tracking apps. They worked, but they were all packed with ads, subscriptions, mandatory accounts, and a data policy that sells whatever it can.

That irked me enough to build Will Fitness myself: an app that exists solely to serve the person using it. Privacy and simplicity come first, without giving up the features that make the big apps useful. I've tried to match what the large fitness apps offer without asking you to pay, sign up, or trade your data for convenience.

— William
